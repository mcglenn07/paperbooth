# Primary Halloween theme

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. Original cheerful orange artwork, designed for children, with pumpkins, candy, friendly spiders, and webs.

The background goes in Paperbooth → Setup → Background. The event card goes in Setup → Title cell. The app scales them into its 1800 × 1200 sheet and 800 × 500 title cell.

## Final generation prompts

### Background

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: full-bleed background artwork for a children's Halloween photobooth sheet, landscape 3:2, intended 1800 x 1200 pixels.
Create an ORIGINAL cheerful orange Halloween repeat pattern for a Primary children's activity. Rich tangerine-orange background, with a charming small-scale scattered pattern of rounded pumpkins with simple happy faces, individually wrapped sweets, candy corn, tiny stars and dots, and occasional tiny friendly smiling spiders with delicate cream spiderwebs. Sophisticated playful hand-cut-paper illustration with very subtle paper texture, crisp clean shapes, warm apricot and cream highlights, burnt-orange outlines and a few dark teal details. Bright, welcoming and joyful, never scary.
Important composition: this is JUST background artwork, no photo placeholders, no rectangles, no title card, no lettering, no text, no logos. Pattern evenly fills every edge and middle; motifs small enough to read in the narrow borders/gutters around photo cells, each pumpkin about 3-4 percent of the canvas width, many small motifs. Avoid large focal objects. Every element is original.
Avoid ghosts of any kind, skulls, skeletons, bats, monsters, witches, haunted houses, tombstones, horror, frightening expressions, dark gloomy lighting. Spiders and webs must look cute and welcoming. No watermark. Output one landscape background image.

### Event title card

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: standalone event title artwork for the fourth cell of a photobooth print. Landscape 8:5 aspect ratio, intended 800 x 500 pixels.
Create an ORIGINAL polished, joyful Halloween event card for a Primary children's activity. Charming dimensional hand-cut-paper illustration with subtle layered paper shadows. A warm creamy apricot central plaque on vivid tangerine orange. Big professionally arranged playful chunky lettering, highly legible at small print size.
Text verbatim, on three centered lines:
"PRIMARY"
"HALLOWEEN"
"ACTIVITY"
Make HALLOWEEN the largest line, friendly curved bold burnt-orange lettering with a restrained cream outline; PRIMARY and ACTIVITY in smaller dark teal lettering. All text fully visible, correctly spelled, no other words, no date, no logo. Center text in the middle 65 percent of the card with generous breathing room.
Around the edges: a cluster of sweet smiling round orange pumpkins at bottom left and bottom right, a few wrapped candies and candy corn, tiny paper stars, playful cream webs in the top corners and ONE small cute smiling dark-teal spider hanging from a thin silk thread near the upper right. Preserve strong text contrast. Small cream scalloped details, joyful orange/apricot/cream palette with tiny deep teal accents. It should feel like a delightful crafted invitation for children, beautifully balanced, bright and welcoming.
Avoid ghosts of any kind, skulls, skeletons, bats, monsters, witches, haunted houses, tombstones, horror, fangs, scary faces, gloomy lighting. No photo cells, no mockup, no outer scene. Full-bleed standalone illustrated title card.
